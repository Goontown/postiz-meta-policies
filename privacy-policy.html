Privacy Policy — Postiz Icarus

Last updated: 2026-10-08

This application ("Postiz Icarus") is a personal, self-hosted social media scheduling tool operated by Joel Lindblom. It uses Meta's Facebook and Instagram APIs to publish content to the operator's own Facebook Page and linked Instagram business account, and to read posting insights for those pages.

What data is processed

  • Content the operator composes for publishing (text, images, videos and scheduling metadata).
  • Page and Instagram business account identifiers, page names, and content insights returned by the Meta APIs.
  • Account access tokens issued via Meta's OAuth grant, stored encrypted on the operator's personal server.

What is not done

  • Data is not shared with, sold to, or visible to any third party.
  • The application processes only the operator's own Facebook Page and linked Instagram business account, as granted by the operator's own OAuth consent.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are used.

Data storage and retention

All data is stored on a private server operated by the account owner and retained until the operator deletes it. Revoking the application's access on Facebook (Settings → Business integrations → remove this app) immediately invalidates the stored tokens, after which no further data is accessed.
