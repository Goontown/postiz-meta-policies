Data Deletion — Postiz Icarus

Last updated: 2026-10-08

This application only processes data belonging to its operator. If you have granted this application access to your Facebook or Instagram account and want your data deleted:

Option 1 — Remove app access (recommended)

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Business integrations (Apps and Websites).
  3. Find Postiz Icarus and select Remove.

This revokes the app's tokens immediately; the operator's server retains no usable access afterwards and cached references are deleted on demand.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email joellindblom90@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Deletion is completed within 30 days and confirmed by reply.
